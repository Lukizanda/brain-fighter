Boss arena layout sketch v3: floating island (Phase 8, greybox proposal)

Top-down, 1 unit = 1 stud, measured from the plaza centre (world X 53.4, Z 28.2; floor top Y 202.5). v2 replaces the disc, the regular rings and the three identical balconies with an irregular island, rock clusters that erode to a bedrock core, and three different ways out past the ridge. The line from the boss spawn to each weak ridge section and each cave plug is kept clear of cover. v3 change: the bridge is gone. Players arrive by a one-way teleporter from the ready room and the ridge is closed all the way round; the only ways off the island are dying or quitting. Shapes are indicative; numbers are starting points. Design page: boss-arena.md.

Three ways out

The Spit (west): a tongue of rock that narrows to a point. Wildcards at the tip; one knock-up and you are off.
The Stones (south-east): three drifting islets across open gaps. A jump each way, with the best letters on the last.
The Overhang (north): a cracked slab that is itself breakable. Boss fire that lands on it drops it for a while.

Two caves

One hollow in the ridge (north-west), one hollow boulder standing mid-field (south). Each has a rock plug facing the boss.
